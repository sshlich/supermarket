# COMPLAINT.TXT
To the council.

A man was found alive in the snow on the 14th, at the end of Merrow Street. He says he walked toward the lights.

I am told the lighting budget for the street is to be cut to nothing next year. I would like it entered in the minutes that the lights are why he is alive.

I do not expect an answer.
