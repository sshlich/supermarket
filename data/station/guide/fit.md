# Fittings (fit) reader's guide

These 26 items are the placed furniture and equipment of your shop: shelves, cabinets, safes, beds, signs, boards and a few devices. Nothing here is crafted, scavenged, sold or consumed. Every one of them is granted by exactly one upgrade, bought with credits, and then placed on the grid.

In the overall loop (scrap -> crusher/recycler -> materials -> machines -> goods -> sold), fittings are an end-of-loop credit sink: the money you earn from selling goods is spent here to buy storage, comfort and shop capability. They have no recipes, no scavenge sites, no shop listing and no orders.

Each entry therefore has a bare Chain. Where the data gives an effect (a description, a note), it is repeated; no further mechanics are claimed. The 'worth' figure is the item's listed value, not the upgrade price, which is given in each entry.

Groups: storage and containers, secure storage, beds and comfort, shop front and information, machine and power fittings, late-game.
